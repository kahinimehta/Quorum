    Minimum 1, maximum 9. Card colors match the scale above. The revise loop typically stops when F1 ≥ 5 and F2 ≥ 5 (or max rounds).

    What do F1, F2 & F3 scores mean?
    F1 — Why this project? Significance, specific knowledge gap, central hypothesis, and innovation. Did the opening argument name a real barrier and cite the corpus?
    F2 — How you’ll do it Rigor per aim: testable hypothesis, concrete methods, pitfalls/alternatives, and expected outcomes. Scored separately for each Specific Aim.
    F3 — Can the team deliver? Feasibility per aim: assumed expertise, equipment, cohort access, and timelines. Surfaces whether the team can actually run the proposed work.
